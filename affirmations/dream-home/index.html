Dream home affirmations

Search for dream home affirmations and you will mostly find promises about the house you are going to get. Mira’s home phrases are not that.

They are about the home you are making: the one you want, described as if it is already yours, and the small things that get you closer in the place you live now. That makes them useful whether you are saving for somewhere, renting, or staying put.

The home you picture

Nothing here says “someday”. They describe the home you want in the present tense, so the line works whether you are saving for a place, renting one, or making the one you have more your own.

Pairs with the dream-home photo itself: the kitchen, the light, the view you would want to wake up to.

Making it yours

About choosing rather than accumulating. “Not a showroom” is the counterweight to every perfect interior you scroll past.

Pairs with a corner you already love, a shelf with only a few things on it, a room that looks lived in.

Small jobs, done today

Deliberately tiny. “And stop” and “not the house” are there to shrink a job until it is small enough to start, which is more use on a Saturday morning than a big vision.

Pairs with open windows, clean sheets, a plant on a sill.

Settling in

For the evenings. These are about using your home rather than improving it; the good chair is there to be sat in.

Pairs with lamplight, a pot on the stove, a sofa at dusk.

Put the picture next to the words

A dream-home board works best with a real photo, a room or a view or a front door, paired with a line from the first group. Mira takes any photo from your library, so save the one you keep coming back to and put it on your Home Screen.
