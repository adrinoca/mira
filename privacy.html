Mira

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Mira does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for anything to be sent to.

What stays on your device

Everything Mira knows about you lives on your iPhone and nowhere else:

  • The photos you add to your board. Mira copies them into its own storage so the widget can show them. They are never uploaded.
  • The phrases you save or write yourself.
  • Your chosen life areas, your widget theme, how often it rotates, and the time of your daily reminder.

All of it sits in a private container shared only between Mira and its own widget. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Mira sends

Nothing. Mira contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs of any kind, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification, scheduled by your iPhone. The phrase it carries is chosen on your device. Nothing about it leaves your phone, and you can turn it off in Mira's settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple. Mira never sees your name, your payment details or your Apple Account — it only records on your device whether a purchase is currently active. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction. You can manage or cancel a subscription from inside Mira's settings, or in iOS Settings under your name → Subscriptions.

Photos

When you add a photo, iOS presents its own picker. Mira receives only the images you choose and never has access to the rest of your library.

Children

Mira is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone of any age.

Your rights

Because Mira holds no data outside your device, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes everything it ever stored.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Mira ever starts collecting anything — analytics, for example — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
