Vision board ideas for men

Plenty of men like the idea of a vision board and never make one because the examples online look like scrapbooks. It does not have to look like that. A board is just a handful of pictures of what you are aiming at, kept somewhere you will see them.

Think of it as a dashboard for the year: a few goals, one picture each, and a line that says why.

Written by the person who makes Mira · Updated October 2026

Career and building something

Picture the work itself: what you are making, where, and with whom. A job title on its own gives you nothing to do on Monday.

  • The project or business you want to ship this year
  • Your workspace set up the way you would like it
  • A skill you want to be properly good at, and the tool that goes with it
  • Someone a few steps ahead whose career you respect

Money

Pick one number and one purpose. A board covered in sports cars says less than a single card with the amount you want saved by December.

  • A savings, debt or investment target written down
  • What the money is for: a house, a business, time off, security for your family
  • A spreadsheet or app screenshot you will actually update

Fitness and health

Use training pictures you could take yourself next week, not physiques from an advert.

  • The route you run, the gym you go to, the bike you ride
  • A race, a lift or a hike you want to complete
  • Sleep: a dark room and an alarm set for the same time every day

People

The area most men leave off, and the one they are often gladdest to have added.

  • A photo with the friends you want to see more of
  • Time with your partner or kids, the way you want it to look
  • A call you keep meaning to make

The thing you keep putting off

Every board needs one picture of the project that has been “next year” for a while: the trip, the instrument, the book, the boat you want to restore. Put it on the board and give it a date.

Where to keep it

If a poster on the wall is not your style, keep the board on your phone. Mira shows one photo and one line at a time as a widget, which looks like part of the phone rather than a collage.

Free printable 2027 templates →

Five pictures is enough

Start with five: one per goal that matters this year. Add more only when one of them is done.
